# T01 — scheduling model contract and reference cases

Status: independent tier-A semantic review accepted and corrections committed as `af43ff1`; push, protected remote CI, and integration pending. Dependencies: none. Branch: `codex/t01-scheduling-contract`.

Deliverable: a source-audited M1 scheduling state machine, exact simultaneous-event and ready-queue rules, dispatch-cost accounting, and independently worked FCFS/RR fixtures. This task defines behavior but implements no scheduler or event kernel.

Owner: primary orchestrator, tier A / high responsibility using the session's inherited settings. Owned implementation paths are `content/concepts/scheduling-m1.md` and `tests/reference/scheduling/`. The owner may also update T01 task/evidence records, the backlog, model-routing decision, and handoff. `crates/`, `apps/`, transport schemas, and package/workspace manifests are out of scope.

Prerequisites and sources: specification sections 6, 9, and 11; OSTEP v1.10 chapter 7 sections 7.1–7.3 and 7.6–7.8; chapter 10 only for the distinction between general multiprocessor concerns and this project's intentionally simplified identical-core/global-queue model. The OSTEP scheduling homework is supplemental and does not override the project model.

Acceptance:

- Every lifecycle transition and its queue/core ownership is defined.
- Ordering covers same-tick completion, switch completion, arrival, I/O completion, quantum expiration, and dispatch without relying on host iteration order.
- FCFS and RR rules cover yield, blocking, completion exactly at a quantum boundary, zero/nonzero switch cost, and multiple cores.
- At least six worked cases cover simultaneous arrivals, quantum/burst equality, nonzero switching, I/O wakeup, idle cores, and two-core dispatch.
- Each case has exact half-open core intervals and independently calculated response, turnaround, ready-wait, dispatch-overhead, blocked-time, and core utilization totals.
- Sourced rules are distinguished from OS Laboratory conventions and omissions.
- A separate tier-A semantic reviewer must approve the contract before T03. Author self-review is useful but is not independent review.
